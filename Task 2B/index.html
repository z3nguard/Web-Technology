ABOUT THE GAME

Think.
Investigate.
Decide.

In ATLAS, you take the role of an investigator. Instead of simply being told what happened, you have to explore the environment and find clues yourself.

Every piece of evidence can change your theory. The goal is to understand the case and identify the person responsible.
